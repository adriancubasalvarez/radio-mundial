Radio Mundial

Español

Internet radio with a touch world map. Install it on your board right here: no software, just your browser and a USB cable.

1Choose your board

2Connect and install

  1. Connect the board to your computer with a USB data cable (some cables only charge).
  2. Click Install, pick the board's port in the browser window and confirm.
  3. Wait until it finishes (1–2 minutes). Don't unplug the board.
Your browser can't install: use Chrome or Edge on a computer (not on a phone). Installing requires a secure page (https).

3Language and Wi-Fi

  • The first time it starts, the radio asks for your language: tap English.
  • When the installation finishes, the same window offers to connect the radio to your Wi-Fi: pick your network and type the password.
  • Or from your phone: if the radio has no Wi-Fi, its screen shows "Connect the radio to your Wi-Fi" and a QR code. Join the RadioMundial-XXXX network with your phone and a page opens to choose your Wi-Fi.
  • To change the Wi-Fi or the language later: press and hold the "Radio Mundial" title on the screen for 2 seconds.

The Wi-Fi must be 2.4 GHz (ESP32 boards don't use 5 GHz).

The board doesn't show up in the list?

  • Try another USB cable or another port on your computer.
  • The CYD uses the CH340 USB chip: on Windows and macOS you may need its official driver.
  • Still not there? Hold the board's BOOT button while you plug it in.
